Public and Affordable Housing Flood Risk

This map shows the extent of storm surge flooding in New York City caused by Hurricane Sandy in 2012, overlaid with the locations of NYCHA public housing developments. Together they show which public housing sites fell within the inundation zone — a question that matters because residents of public and affordable housing often have the fewest resources to recover from a flood.

Data sources: Sandy Inundation Zone and NYCHA Public Housing Developments Map, both from NYC Open Data. Basemap by Mapbox.
